## What are the requirements?

We expect very specific interests in our research areas (ideally stemming from prior experience such as research assistant or research visit with us). Related publications may help our review but is not mandatory.

## What to include?

You should include a detailed CV, your academic transcripts, a research statement outlining your specific interests and how they align with our group's work, and any relevant publications or research experience. Letters of recommendation would be beneficial at the stage of fellowship applications.

## Funding and support

We refer to several competitive fellowships and scholarships: Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS), International PhD Fellowship Scheme (IPFS), and PolyU Research Postgraduate Scholarship (PRPgS).
