## What does this cover?

Independent postdoctoral research with a high degree of autonomy, acquisition and managing of research projects, contributing to the collaborative environment of the group, mentoring of students, and participation in funding applications.

## What are the requirements?

An excellent PhD thesis and existing research relevant to our group are essential.

## What to include?

You should include a detailed CV, a comprehensive research statement outlining your past work and future research plans, a list of publications, and contact information for references. Sample publications or your PhD thesis are also welcome.

## Funding and support

While we do not have open positions or guaranteed funding available for every inquiry, there are various sources of funding that we explore on a case-by-case basis: e.g., PolyU Postdoctoral Fellowships Scheme (PDF Scheme), Hong Kong Scholars Program.
